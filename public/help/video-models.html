Video models

Two lists on this page. The form asks for an intent first, then a system, then a model from a family.

Models

The family that draws the clip — Wan, LTX, MiniMax H3. One family can run more than one intent. Replicate adds other labs for Text to Video and Image to Video.

Intents

The job. Invent a clip, make a still move, talk to speech, copy motion or edit a clip you already have, or hold refs. The form calls these intents.

In the form

  1. In Editor, add a video clip or click Add asset.
  2. Choose the intent: Text to Video, Image to Video, Audio to Video, Video to Video, or Refs to Video.
  3. Choose a system: Parascene (credits), Direct to Blue, or Replicate (your token).
  4. Choose the model that matches that intent.
  5. Fill what the intent asks for — frames, source audio, a driving clip, or refs — then Generate.

Parascene today is Wan and LTX for Text to Video and Image to Video, plus LTX Audio to Video (ltx_a2v). Direct to Blue is the full Blue catalog, including MiniMax H3, Video to Video, and Refs to Video. Replicate does not run Audio to Video, Video to Video, or Refs to Video yet.

Models

Same family, different recipes. The codes in the list are what the form shows after you pick the intent.

Wan

Fast. Text to Video and Image to Video stay silent unless you add audio yourself. On Direct to Blue, Wan also edits a clip you already have.

  • Wan — text-to-video (rapid AIO) — wan_t2v
  • Wan — image-to-video — wan_i2v (first frame, or first and last)
  • Wan — Bernini-R video edit — bernini_r_v2v
  • Wan — Animate 2 — wan_animate (needs a character still)

LTX

Often writes audio with the picture. This is the family for a talking clip — the one we used after the goblin still.

  • LTX — text-to-video — ltx_t2v
  • LTX — image-to-video — ltx_i2v
  • LTX — style transition — ltx_style_transition (first and last, plus a LoRA)
  • LTX — audio-to-video (ia2v) — ltx_a2v
  • LTX — ID-LoRA talkvid — ltx_id_lora
  • LTX — IC-LoRA video control — ltx_ic_lora (needs a character still)
  • LTX — IC-LoRA ingredients — ltx_ingredients

MiniMax H3

Strong native audio (FL2VA / Ref2VA). These do not appear under Parascene — use Direct to Blue, or Replicate for Text to Video and Image to Video.

  • MiniMax H3 — text-to-video (FL2VA) — minimax_t2v
  • MiniMax H3 — image-to-video (FL2VA) — minimax_i2v (first frame, or first and last)
  • MiniMax H3 — reference-to-video (Ref2VA) — minimax_r2v

Intents

Pick the job. The model list shrinks to the recipes that can do it.

Text to Video

Invent a clip from a prompt. No still, no existing video — you want a new shot from words. The clip can land on the timeline or in Assets. Parascene: Wan or LTX. Direct to Blue adds MiniMax H3. Replicate is MiniMax H3, Seedance, Kling, Veo, Vidu, and the other video models you have enabled.

Image to Video

Make a still move. Keep the face and the room; add camera and motion. First is the start frame. Last is optional — when both are set, Wan, MiniMax H3, and LTX style transition can land on the second still.

On Replicate you can also motion match: keep the character from a still, and copy the motion from the previous clip on the timeline.

Audio to Video

Turn speech you already have into a talking clip. The still is the face; the audio on the timeline is the timing. Source audio is full mix or vocals. The clip is shorter than the speech — later clips fill the rest the same way.

Parascene and Direct to Blue: ltx_a2v. Direct to Blue also has ID-LoRA talkvid. That is the clip in Generate a video with audio.

Video to Video

You already have a clip. Copy the motion from it, or edit the clip itself — restyle it, recast who is in it, or keep the same camera. Some models also want a character still so the new clip stays on that person. Direct to Blue: Bernini-R, Wan Animate 2, and LTX IC-LoRA.

Refs to Video

Hold onto who, what, and where, then write a new clip that stays on those pieces. Stills — and sometimes clips or audio — are references, not a finished shot. Then a prompt.

MiniMax H3 Ref2VA takes up to nine stills, three clips, and three audio files. LTX ingredients is the other recipe. Both run on Direct to Blue.

Where to go from here
