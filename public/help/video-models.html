Video models

You pick an intent first — what you have, and what you want back — then a system, then a model. Wan, LTX, and MiniMax H3 show up under more than one intent. Replicate adds other labs for Text to Video and Image to Video.

In the form

  1. In Editor, add a video clip or click Add asset.
  2. Choose the intent: Text to Video, Image to Video, Audio to Video, Video to Video, or Refs to Video.
  3. Choose a system: Parascene (credits), Direct to Blue, or Replicate (your token).
  4. Choose the model that matches that intent.
  5. Fill what the intent asks for — frames, source audio, a driving clip, or refs — then Generate.

Parascene today is Wan and LTX for Text to Video and Image to Video, plus LTX Audio to Video (ltx_a2v). Direct to Blue is the full Blue catalog, including MiniMax H3, Video to Video, and Refs to Video. Replicate does not run Audio to Video, Video to Video, or Refs to Video yet.

Wan

Fast. Text to Video and Image to Video stay silent unless you add audio yourself. On Direct to Blue, Wan also edits a clip you already have.

  • Wan — text-to-video (rapid AIO) — wan_t2v
  • Wan — image-to-video — wan_i2v (first frame, or first and last)
  • Wan — Bernini-R video edit — bernini_r_v2v
  • Wan — Animate 2 — wan_animate (needs a character still)

LTX

Often writes audio with the picture. This is the family for a talking clip — the one we used after the goblin still.

  • LTX — text-to-video — ltx_t2v
  • LTX — image-to-video — ltx_i2v
  • LTX — style transition — ltx_style_transition (first and last, plus a LoRA)
  • LTX — audio-to-video (ia2v) — ltx_a2v
  • LTX — ID-LoRA talkvid — ltx_id_lora
  • LTX — IC-LoRA video control — ltx_ic_lora (needs a character still)
  • LTX — IC-LoRA ingredients — ltx_ingredients

MiniMax H3

Strong native audio (FL2VA / Ref2VA). These do not appear under Parascene — use Direct to Blue, or Replicate for Text to Video and Image to Video.

  • MiniMax H3 — text-to-video (FL2VA) — minimax_t2v
  • MiniMax H3 — image-to-video (FL2VA) — minimax_i2v (first frame, or first and last)
  • MiniMax H3 — reference-to-video (Ref2VA) — minimax_r2v

Text to Video

A prompt. No still. The clip can land on the timeline or in Assets. Parascene: Wan or LTX. Direct to Blue adds MiniMax H3. Replicate is MiniMax H3, Seedance, Kling, Veo, Vidu, and the other video models you have enabled.

Image to Video

A still becomes motion. First is the start frame. Last is optional — when both are set, Wan, MiniMax H3, and LTX style transition can land on the second still.

On Replicate you can also motion match: a character still plus the previous clip on the timeline as the motion.

Audio to Video

Speech or a mix is already on the audio lane. First frame is the face. Source audio is full mix or vocals. The clip is shorter than the speech — later clips fill the rest the same way.

Parascene and Direct to Blue: ltx_a2v. Direct to Blue also has ID-LoRA talkvid. That is the clip in Generate a video with audio.

Video to Video

A clip you already have, plus a prompt. Some models also want a character still. Direct to Blue: Bernini-R, Wan Animate 2, and LTX IC-LoRA.

Refs to Video

Stills — and sometimes clips or audio — as references: who is in the shot, what they wear, where they are. Then a prompt.

MiniMax H3 Ref2VA takes up to nine stills, three clips, and three audio files. LTX ingredients is the other recipe. Both run on Direct to Blue.
