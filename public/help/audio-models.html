Audio models

Two jobs. Speak a line, or write a score. The form asks for an intent first, then a system, then a model.

Models

Four models. Speech is Gemini Flash TTS or MiniMax Speech. Music is Lyria 3 or MiniMax Music. The codes in the lists are what the form shows after you pick the intent.

Intents

The job. Text to Speech or Text to Music. Turning speech into a talking clip is a video job — not this list.

In the form

  1. In Editor, click Add asset.
  2. Choose the intent: Text to Speech or Text to Music.
  3. Choose a system: Parascene (credits) or Replicate (your token).
  4. Choose the model that matches that intent.
  5. Fill the line or the score prompt, then Generate. The clip lands in Assets.

Parascene and Replicate share the same four models. Direct to Blue is coming soon for speech and music.

Text to Speech

Speak a line. You type the words; the model picks a voice. Use this when you want narration, a replacement line, or talk to drop on the timeline. Gemini Flash TTS is the default.

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS

Cheap narration. Many named voices — Kore, Puck, and the rest. Optional Style steers how it speaks. This is the model for a first spoken line.

  • Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS — google/gemini-3.1-flash-tts

MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo

System voices plus Custom. Emotion sets the delivery. Custom uses a voice_id you already have — including ids from MiniMax’s system list that are not in the Voice dropdown — or Train voice from a spoken file in Assets. Training is optional and uses more credits than a line of speech. Hear the English ids, pauses, and emotion on Speech voices.

  • MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo — minimax/speech-2.8-turbo

Text to Music

Write a score. You describe the music; the model writes audio. Use this when you want a bed under a scene, or a song. Lyria 3 is the default.

Lyria 3

Instrumental score from a prompt. About thirty seconds. Good for a dramatic bed — no lyrics field.

  • Lyria 3 — google/lyria-3

MiniMax Music 2.6

Song or MV track. Add Lyrics, or turn on Instrumental. Lyrics optimizer can tidy the words before it sings.

  • MiniMax Music 2.6 — minimax/music-2.6

Where to go from here

Speech voices — the same line on Gemini and MiniMax, Custom ids, pauses, and emotion.

Generate speech and music — one spoken line, then a second clip on A2.

Generate a video with audio — turn speech you already have into a talking clip. The models for that job are on Video models.

Image models — the stills those clips start from.
